Extended Character Stats
========================

Extra combat stats beside your character sheet, for vanilla on SoloCraft.

Open the character window. The ECS button shows and hides the panel.
Settings is at the top of that panel. /ecs toggle and /ecs config do
the same.

Ranged stats are hidden for paladins and shamans. Mana is hidden for
warriors and rogues. Block appears while you have a shield. Parry
appears for classes that can parry.

Hit on the panel is a percentage. Defense is your defense skill. Haste
is casting speed from buffs such as Mind Quickening.
