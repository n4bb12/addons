Compared with 3.0.9
==========================

Spell crit
----------

Spell crit
    3.0.9
        One level-60 ratio. A mage with 382 intellect is about 6.6%.
        A paladin is 1% per 29.5 intellect. 3.0.9 attributes those
        ratios to the Wowhead Classic gear planner.

    This addon
        base + intellect / (rate0 + rate1 × level). The base and the
        two rates change by class, and the second rate grows with
        level. That mage is about 10.8%.

    Why
        vMaNGOS Unit::GetSpellCritFromIntellect is the roll the 1.12
        server uses. The level-60 ratio is a shortcut, and the
        paladin ratio does not match that roll.

Devastation
    3.0.9
        1% spell crit per rank on every school.

    This addon
        Fire crit only.

    Why
        The talent is every Destruction spell. Shadow Bolt and
        Shadowburn are Destruction. The Affliction shadow spells are
        not, so a shadow line would include the bonus on spells that
        do not have it.

Force of Will
    3.0.9
        1% spell crit on every school, and 1% spell damage per rank.

    This addon
        Not on the school lines.

    Why
        It covers some offensive spells only, so a school line would
        include the bonus on spells that do not have it. The damage
        percent is separate from flat spell damage.

Tidal Mastery
    3.0.9
        Not included.

    This addon
        1% nature crit per rank.

    Why
        Healing and lightning are nature.

Combustion
    3.0.9
        Named, and the stacks are not added to fire crit. The bonus
        is stored as spell crit for one school, and the buff loop
        only reads a buff's all-schools spell crit.

    This addon
        10% fire crit per stack.

    Why
        The buff is fire crit, and each stack adds another 10%.

Fire Blast
    3.0.9
        Not included.

    This addon
        +50% fire crit while the buff is on you.

    Why
        The buff is fire crit.

Songflower, Slip'kik's Savvy, mage Power of the Guardian, Brilliant Wizard Oil
    3.0.9
        Songflower is +5% spell crit, Slip'kik's Savvy is +3%, the
        mage Power of the Guardian is +2%, and Brilliant Wizard Oil
        is +1%.

    This addon
        The same four.

    Why
        Spell crit is calculated, so these bonuses have to be added
        while the buff or oil is up. Melee crit and ranged crit
        already include Songflower's 5%.

Spell damage, hit, attack power, and block
------------------------------------------

Spell damage and healing
    3.0.9
        Rebuilt from gear, then multiplied by percent talents such as
        Shadow Mastery and Spiritual Healing.

    This addon
        The flat spell damage on your character. Healing is that
        number, plus healing-only gear and healing-only buffs.
        GetSpellBonusHealing() stays 0.

    Why
        Checked in game: with Shadow Mastery at 5/5, spell damage
        reads the same number on every school, so those percents are
        not in the field. Spiritual Healing multiplies the heal when
        it lands. It is not +healing. GetSpellBonusHealing() stays 0
        because the 1.12 object has no healing-done field.

Hit
    3.0.9
        GetHitModifier() is treated as broken and is not used. Arcane
        Focus, Elemental Precision, and Shadow Focus are not added.
        Surefooted is ranged only. Against a boss, miss starts at 9%
        and then another 1% of hit is removed when weapon skill is
        under 305. Ranged hit is
        GetCombatRatingBonus(CR_HIT_RANGED). A vanilla character has
        no hit rating, so ranged hit shows 0.

    This addon
        GetHitModifier(), plus those talents on the schools they
        cover, and Surefooted on melee and ranged. Ranged hit uses
        that same hit chance, the Biznicks scope, and hit on gear.
        Against a boss, miss is 8% with no hit. The first 1% of hit
        does not lower that 8%.

    Why
        Hermes forwards some hit auras, so the client hit is kept.
        Surefooted has no weapon restriction. Arcane Focus is arcane,
        Elemental Precision is fire and frost, and Shadow Focus is
        shadow. The 9% boss miss already includes the 1% of hit
        vanilla ignores, so removing it again counts that 1% twice.

Ranged attack power
    3.0.9
        The hunter, rogue, or warrior base, and one stack of Jom
        Gabbar. Set bonuses, "Attack Power +26 and +1% Critical
        Strike", and Aspect of the Hawk are not counted.

    This addon
        That same base, plus gear, enchants, set bonuses, and the
        ranged buffs. Cryptstalker 4-piece is +50. Jom Gabbar is
        counted per stack. Every other class is agility − 10. A
        druid in cat or bear has no base from level or agility.
        UnitRangedAttackPower is 0 for every class, so this sum is
        what the row shows. A client value above 0 is kept.

    Why
        The 1.12 object has no ranged attack power field.
        Cryptstalker and that shoulder enchant belong in the total,
        and each Jom Gabbar stack is its own bonus. The base is
        vMaNGOS Unit::GetAttackPowerFromStrengthAndAgility.

Melee attack power and defense
    3.0.9
        A formula when attack power is 0. Defense below 5 per level
        is raised up to 5 per level.

    This addon
        The attack power and defense skill on your character.

    Why
        UnitAttackPower and UnitDefense read fields on the 1.12
        object, and Hermes fills them. A low attack power or a low
        defense skill is the number you actually have.

Block value
    3.0.9
        Strength / 20, and paladin Shield Specialization only.

    This addon
        (shield and gear block + strength / 20 − 1) × Shield
        Specialization. The result is never below 0, and it is cut to
        a whole number. Shaman Shield Specialization is 5% per rank.
        Paladin Shield Specialization is 10% per rank.

    Why
        vMaNGOS Player::GetShieldBlockValue. The minus 1 comes before
        the talent. Before the talent, 3.0.9 is 1 higher.

Crit reduction, avoidance, and mana
-----------------------------------

Crit reduction
    3.0.9
        No crit reduction row.

    This addon
        1% per 25 defense above 300 at level 60 for melee and ranged,
        plus buffs and Sleight of Hand. Spell crit reduction comes
        from buffs.

    Why
        Defense skill reduces melee and ranged crit taken. In
        vanilla, defense does not reduce spell crit.

Avoidance
    3.0.9
        One figure, against an attacker of your level, from the raw
        client dodge, parry, and block.

    This addon
        One row against your level and one against a boss. Against a
        boss, dodge, parry, and block in that total are each 0.6%
        lower. Enemy miss has the same two rows.

    Why
        The dodge, parry, and block rows are against your level.
        Enemy miss is 5% plus 0.04% per point of defense above the
        attacker's weapon skill.

Spirit mana
    3.0.9
        The first 50 spirit at 0.25 mana per second, then a class
        rate. Shaman is 0.1. Druid is 0.1125.

    This addon
        Vanilla's regen, spirit / divisor + a flat amount, shown as
        mana per 5. A shaman's flat amount is 17 mana per 2 seconds.

    Why
        vMaNGOS Unit::GetRegenMPPerSpirit. Above 50 spirit the two
        rates match, except a shaman in 3.0.9 is 1 mana per second
        low and a druid is high. GetManaRegen() has that same shaman
        gap, and it is low for every class below level 60.

Earthshatterer
    3.0.9
        4 pieces, +25% Mana Spring, and no 8-piece.

    This addon
        4 pieces, +25% Mana Spring, and 8 pieces, +15 mana per 5
        while Lightning Shield is up.

    Why
        Both bonuses are part of the set.

Blessing of Wisdom
    3.0.9
        Scaled by your own Improved Blessing of Wisdom.

    This addon
        The mana amount written on the buff, while casting and while
        not casting.

    Why
        The talent belongs to the paladin who cast the buff, so your
        own points are the wrong scale when someone else cast it.
        MP5 (Buffs) is flat mana per 5, so Wisdom sits on the casting
        rows.

Glancing, schools, and haste
    3.0.9
        No glancing rows. One spell hit for every school, one spell
        crit for every school. Haste is hidden, and the value behind
        it is 0.

    This addon
        Glancing chance and glancing damage, against your level and
        against a boss. Spell hit, spell miss, and spell crit each
        have a line per school. Haste is casting speed from buffs
        such as Mind Quickening.

    Why
        Glancing is a melee roll. A school talent covers one school.
        Haste rating is not a vanilla stat. Casting speed from a
        buff is.

Figures players asked for on 3.0.9
----------------------------------

Spell crit
    Note
        Mage crit per intellect showed 59.2, and should be 59.5.

    Why that figure is wrong
        Both numbers are level-60 ratios. 3.0.9 says they come from
        the Wowhead Classic gear planner, and 59.2 is that planner's
        shaman ratio. Neither is the crit the 1.12 server rolls.

    This addon
        base + intellect / (rate0 + rate1 × level). The base and the
        two rates change by class, and the second rate grows with
        level.

    Why
        At 60 a mage is about 53.8 intellect per 1%, plus a 3.70%
        base, so 382 intellect is about 10.8%.

    Source
        vMaNGOS Unit::GetSpellCritFromIntellect.

Healing power
    Note
        1358 healing on gear and 393 spirit showed as 1602, and
        should be 1456 (0.25 × 393 + 1358).

    Why that figure is wrong
        1602 is 1456 × 1.10. Spiritual Healing 5/5 had been applied
        to healing power, and that talent multiplies the heal when it
        lands.

    This addon
        Shared spell damage plus healing-only gear. Spiritual Healing
        is not applied. Spiritual Guidance is already inside spell
        damage, and it is not added a second time.

    Why
        25% of spirit is cut to a whole number. Checked on Cloetta:
        Spiritual Guidance 5/5, 358 spirit, Atiesh +120, and every
        school reads 209. floor(358 × 0.25) is 89.

    Source
        vMaNGOS SpellCaster::SpellBaseDamageBonusDone adds
        spirit-percent auras to the spell damage field.
        GetSpellBonusHealing() stays 0. The 1.12 object has no
        PLAYER_FIELD_MOD_HEALING_DONE_POS field.

Spell damage and frost enchants
    Note
        A weapon "spell damage +30" and the frost-only lines were not
        counted.

    Why that figure is wrong
        A flat bonus on the character belongs on the panel.

    This addon
        No tooltip scan. Spell damage is GetSpellBonusDamage(), and
        that field already includes the +30. A frost enchant is the
        extra in parentheses on the frost line. Checked in game:
        Frost Damage +20 on gloves is the frost line only, as in
        733 (+20).

    Why
        The server writes each flat spell damage bonus it applies
        into that field. The +30 is shared by every magic school.
        The +20 is frost only.

    Source
        GetSpellBonusDamage(), PLAYER_FIELD_MOD_DAMAGE_DONE_POS, and
        vMaNGOS Player::UpdateSpellDamageAndHealingBonus.

Blessing of Wisdom
    Note
        Blessing of Wisdom did not show.

    Why that figure is wrong
        The buff restores mana, so the mana totals were short.

    This addon
        Ranks 1-6 and Greater Blessing of Wisdom ranks 1-2, read from
        the buff, added while casting and while not casting.

    Why
        MP5 (Buffs) is flat mana per 5. Wisdom is on the two mana
        totals.

    Source
        The mana amount on the buff tooltip.

Block value
    Note
        The block value on 3.0.9 was wrong.

    Why that figure is wrong
        Strength / 20 is 1 higher before the talent, and shaman
        Shield Specialization was left out.

    This addon
        (shield and gear block + strength / 20 − 1) × Shield
        Specialization, never below 0, cut to a whole number. Shaman
        is 5% per rank.

    Why
        The server subtracts 1 after strength / 20, then applies the
        talent.

    Source
        vMaNGOS Player::GetShieldBlockValue.

Ranged attack power
    Note
        It showed 0, so Cryptstalker and the shoulder enchant could
        not be checked.

    Why that figure is wrong
        UnitRangedAttackPower is 0 for every class on SoloCraft. The
        1.12 object has no ranged attack power field, so Cryptstalker
        and the shoulder enchant had nothing to sit on.

    This addon
        Built when that call is 0. The class base, plus gear,
        Cryptstalker 4-piece (+50), "Attack Power +26", and the
        ranged buffs. Hunter is level × 2 + agility × 2 − 10. Rogue
        and warrior are level + agility − 10. Every other class is
        agility − 10. A druid in cat or bear has no base from level
        or agility. A client value above 0 is kept.

    Why
        That base is the ranged attack power before gear. The gear
        and buffs sit on top of it.

    Source
        vMaNGOS Unit::GetAttackPowerFromStrengthAndAgility.

Ranged hit
    Note
        Hunter ranged hit was missing, including the Zul'Gurub helm
        and leg enchant.

    Why that figure is wrong
        3.0.9 read ranged hit from hit rating, which a vanilla
        character does not have, and it did not count that enchant.

    This addon
        Your hit, plus Surefooted, the Biznicks scope, hit on gear,
        and "Ranged Attack Power +24/Stamina +10/Hit +1%".

    Why
        That enchant is ranged hit only. Surefooted is 1% per rank
        and has no weapon restriction.

    Source
        GetHitModifier(), the Surefooted talent text, and that
        enchant line.

Melee hit
    Note
        Melee hit was wrong.

    Why that figure is wrong
        Nature's Guidance, Precision, and Surefooted were missing
        from the total.

    This addon
        Your hit, plus those three talents, plus the hit lines on
        gear.

    Why
        Hermes already forwards the hit auras it has. Those talents
        are 1% hit per rank, from the talent text, and
        GetHitModifier() does not include them.

    Source
        GetHitModifier(), and those talent texts.

Spell hit
    Note
        Spell hit was wrong. The mage Zul'Gurub "Spell Hit +1%" was
        already counted.

    Why that figure is wrong
        GetSpellHitModifier() does not include the school talents.

    This addon
        Your spell hit, plus that school's talents, plus "Spell Hit
        +1%" and "chance to hit with spells by N%".

    Why
        Arcane Focus is arcane. Elemental Precision is fire and
        frost. Shadow Focus is shadow.

    Source
        GetSpellHitModifier(), plus the talent text.

Spell penetration
    Note
        Spell penetration was wrong.

    Why that figure is wrong
        GetSpellPenetration() stays 0. The 1.12 object has no
        mod-target-resistance field, so Hermes never fills it.

    This addon
        The green line "decreases the magical resistances of your
        spell targets by N", plus 5 per rank of Arcane Subtlety.

    Why
        The green line is the penetration the server applies. The
        talent adds 5 per rank.

    Source
        The equip tooltip, and the Arcane Subtlety talent text.

Healing enchants
    Note
        Healing enchants were not counted.

    Why that figure is wrong
        GetSpellBonusHealing() stays 0, so a healing enchant has no
        client field to show up in.

    This addon
        "Healing Spells +55", "Healing +31 and 5 mana per 5 sec.",
        and Prophetic Aura.

    Why
        Those lines are healing that is not already inside spell
        damage.

    Source
        Those tooltip lines. The missing field is
        PLAYER_FIELD_MOD_HEALING_DONE_POS.

Frost and fire spell damage
    Note
        +frost should not be increased by the 6% talent, and Fire
        Power is the same kind of talent.

    Why that figure is wrong
        Piercing Ice and Fire Power multiply the finished spell.

    This addon
        The frost line is the flat frost total. A school line shows
        the amount above shared spell damage in parentheses, as in
        624 (+46). Those talents are not added on top.

    Why
        With Shadow Mastery at 5/5, spell damage reads the same
        number on every school.

    Source
        GetSpellBonusDamage().

Character totals
    Note
        Crit, mana per 5 from spirit, mana per 5 on gear, defense,
        armor, and attribute enchants were already fine.

    Why that figure is wrong
        Spirit mana is the exception. 3.0.9 is 1 mana per second low
        for a shaman and high for a druid. The character sheet's own
        spirit regen has that same shaman gap, and it is low for
        every class below level 60.

    This addon
        Melee crit, armor, defense, and attributes stay the
        character's own totals. Spirit mana uses vanilla's regen.

    Why
        Those totals already include gear and enchants.

    Source
        vMaNGOS Unit::GetRegenMPPerSpirit. A 2 second tick is
        spirit / divisor + a flat amount.
