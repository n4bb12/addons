Changelog
=========

This SoloCraft version starts from Extended Character Stats 5.5.2
(https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/extended-character-stats/files/8646156)
by BreakBB and Ethulu. The same release is on GitHub
(https://github.com/BreakBB/ExtendedCharacterStats/releases/tag/v5.5.2).

Kept from 5.5.2
---------------

* Mana regen is added once. 5.3.8 stopped adding it a second time.

* Shadow Focus hit is correct at 4 and 5 points. 5.4.3 fixed those
  two ranks.

* Spell damage shows the bonus shared by every magic school, and
  enemy miss has its own rows. 5.5.0 added those rows and corrected
  boss avoidance.

* A spell-crit buff is added once. 5.5.2 fixed buffs that had been
  added twice.

* Glancing chance and glancing damage were already on the Classic
  page of 5.5.2. So were Combustion, the Fire Blast buff, Benediction,
  and five pieces of The Ten Storms, all inside spell crit.

* Mana kept while casting was already included for Meditation, Arcane
  Meditation, and Reflection. The Earthshatterer 8-piece was too:
  15 mana per 5 while Lightning Shield is up.

Removed from 5.5.2
------------------

The Classic page of 5.5.2 already hides hit rating, haste rating,
expertise, armor penetration, resilience, and defense rating. This
version leaves those rows out. Hit is a percentage. Defense is your
defense skill. Haste is casting speed from buffs such as Mind
Quickening.

5.5.2 still applied a few later-game effects when a character had
them. This version leaves them out.

* Season of Discovery rune engravings, and the extra haste on
  timeworn items.
* Wrath of the Lich King glyph effects, such as a glyph changing
  Barkskin.
* Mana per 5 from socketed gems.
* Balance of Power, Survival of the Fittest, and the shaman talent
  Elemental Precision. A vanilla character cannot take those talents.
  The mage talent Elemental Precision is still included.

Changed for SoloCraft
---------------------

5.5.2 reads several of these numbers from the client. On SoloCraft
those client numbers are 0, so this version calculates them. A few of
5.5.2's own formulas are different too.

Melee and ranged miss
    At 300 weapon skill against a boss, miss is 8% with no hit.
    5.5.2 showed 9%. The first 1% of hit does not lower that 8%, and
    9% hit clears it. Dual wield adds 19%. Miss stays between 0% and
    60%, and drops against a creature below level 10.

    In cat or bear form, weapon skill counts as 5 per level. Miss is
    5% against your level and 8% against a boss, before hit. 5.5.2
    used 9% for cat and bear against a boss, and an off-hand swing
    could still add 19%.

Spell miss
    Still starts at 4% against your level and 17% against a boss, and
    it still cannot fall below 1%. It now stops at 78%. 5.5.2 stopped
    at 90%.

Melee hit
    Adds Surefooted, paladin Precision, and the hit written on your
    gear. 5.5.2 already added Nature's Guidance and rogue Precision.

Ranged hit
    Adds Surefooted, hit on your gear, and ranged-only hit such as
    the "+1% Hit" on Falcon's Call. 5.5.2 already counted the
    Biznicks 247x128 Accurascope.

Spell hit
    Adds the spell-hit lines on your gear. 5.5.2 already added Arcane
    Focus, Shadow Focus, mage Elemental Precision, and Nature's
    Guidance.

Spell crit
    Built from intellect. A level 60 mage with 382 intellect is about
    10.8%. Warriors, rogues, and hunters gain none from intellect.
    This version also adds Songflower Serenade, Slip'kik's Savvy, the
    mage Power of the Guardian, Brilliant Wizard Oil, Rallying Cry of
    the Dragonslayer, Moonkin Aura, and Tidal Mastery. Tidal Mastery
    is 1% nature crit per rank for healing and lightning. Devastation
    stays on fire crit only, as in 5.5.2.

Healing power
    The shared spell damage, plus healing-only gear, plus the priest
    Power of the Guardian. 5.5.2 used the client's healing number,
    which is 0 on SoloCraft.

Block value
    Your shield and other block on your gear, plus strength / 20,
    minus 1. Paladin Shield Specialization then adds 10% per rank, or
    shaman Shield Specialization adds 5% per rank. 5.5.2 used the
    client's block value, which is 0 on SoloCraft.

Spell penetration
    Shown here. The Classic page of 5.5.2 did not show it. It is the
    green line on your gear that lowers a target's resistances, plus
    5 per rank of Arcane Subtlety.

Ranged attack power
    Calculated, because the client number is 0 for every class on
    SoloCraft. A hunter is level × 2 + agility × 2 − 10. A rogue or
    warrior is level + agility − 10. Every other class is agility −
    10. A druid in cat or bear gets none of that base. Gear, enchants,
    set bonuses, and ranged buffs are added on top: Aspect of the
    Hawk, Trueshot Aura, Earthstrike, Slayer's Crest, and each stack
    of Jom Gabbar.

Spirit mana
    Uses vanilla's regen. A shaman's flat amount is 17 mana per 2
    seconds. The character sheet's own spirit regen is 1 mana per
    second low for a shaman, and low for every class below level 60.

Earthshatterer
    The 4-piece adds 25% to Mana Spring.
