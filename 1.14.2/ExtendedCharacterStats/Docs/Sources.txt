Sources
=======

Talents, buffs, and items that change a number on the panel.

Included means the number on the panel contains this effect.

Each school has one number. That number is for bonuses that cover the
whole school.

Melee crit, ranged crit, attack power, armor, dodge, parry, and block
chance already include the talents the game applies to them. This
window calculates spell crit, spell hit, Nature's Guidance, Precision,
Surefooted, spell penetration, block value, and crit reduction.

Miss and glancing use your weapon skill against the target's defense.
A low skill is a high miss chance.

Talents
-------

Druid
-----

Gift of Nature [not included]
    Makes the heal larger. Healing on the panel is the bonus on your
    gear.

Improved Moonfire [not included]
    Moonfire crit only. The nature line would claim it for every
    nature spell.

Improved Regrowth [not included]
    Regrowth crit only.

Moonkin Form [not included]
    The talent shapeshifts you and brings up Moonkin Aura. The crit
    is on the aura, while it is up.

Reflection [included]
    Each rank keeps 5% of your spirit regen while you are casting.

Hunter
------

Surefooted [included]
    Each rank is 1% hit, on melee and ranged. Dodge stays in your
    dodge chance.

Killer Instinct [included]
    Each rank is 1% crit on melee and ranged. Your crit chance
    already includes it.

Mage
----

Arcane Focus [included]
    Each rank is 2% arcane hit. Fire and frost stay as they are.

Arcane Instability [included]
    Each rank is 1% spell crit, on every school.

Arcane Meditation [included]
    Each rank keeps 5% of your spirit regen while you are casting.

Arcane Subtlety [included]
    Each rank is 5 spell penetration.

Critical Mass [included]
    Each rank is 2% fire crit.

Elemental Precision [included]
    Each rank is 2% fire hit and 2% frost hit. Arcane hit stays on
    Arcane Focus.

Improved Arcane Explosion [not included]
    Arcane Explosion crit only.

Improved Flamestrike [not included]
    Flamestrike crit only.

Incinerate [not included]
    The extra crit is on Fire Blast and Scorch only. The fire line
    would claim it for every fire spell.

Winter's Chill [not included]
    A debuff on the target, +2% frost crit taken per stack. Your own
    frost crit does not move.

Paladin
-------

Conviction [included]
    Melee crit, and your crit chance already includes it.

Divine Favor [not included]
    Your next holy spell is a guaranteed crit. One charge.

Holy Power [included]
    Each rank is 1% holy crit.

Precision [included]
    Each rank is 1% melee hit. Paladin Precision covers melee weapons.

Purification [not included]
    Makes the heal larger. Healing on the panel is the bonus on your
    gear.

Shield Specialization [included]
    Each rank is 10% more block value, so the ranks are 10%, 20%, and
    30% more. That bonus is applied before the value is cut to a
    whole number.

Priest
------

Darkness [not included]
    Each rank is 2% more shadow damage. The shadow line is your flat
    spell power. Darkness multiplies the finished spell, including
    its own base damage, so folding it into spell power would be the
    wrong number.

Force of Will [not included]
    Each rank is 1% damage and 1% crit on Smite, Holy Fire, Holy
    Nova, Mind Blast, Mana Burn, and Touch of Weakness damage. It
    does not cover other heals, Power Word: Shield, Mind Flay,
    Shadow Word: Pain, or Devouring Plague. Holy crit and shadow crit
    are each one number for the whole school, so Force of Will is not
    added to those lines.

Holy Specialization [included]
    Each rank is 1% holy crit.

Inner Focus [not included]
    Your next spell is a guaranteed crit and costs no mana. One
    charge.

Meditation [included]
    Each rank keeps 5% of your spirit regen while you are casting.

Spiritual Guidance [included]
    Each rank adds 5% of your spirit to spell damage, as a whole
    number. It is already inside your spell damage.

Shadow Focus [included]
    Each rank is 2% shadow hit, for the whole shadow school.

Spiritual Healing [not included]
    Makes the heal larger. Healing on the panel is the bonus on your
    gear.

Rogue
-----

Precision [included]
    Each rank is 1% melee hit. This is a different talent from the
    paladin one.

Sleight of Hand [included]
    Each rank is 1% less chance to be crit by melee and ranged
    attacks. It is added to melee and ranged crit reduction.

Shaman
------

Call of Thunder [not included]
    The crit is for Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning only: 1%, 2%,
    3%, 4%, then 6%. The nature line is also your heal line, so this
    would overstate Healing Wave, Lesser Healing Wave, and Chain Heal.

Elemental Mastery [not included]
    Your next fire, frost, or nature damage spell is a guaranteed
    crit and costs no mana. One charge.

Elemental Precision [not included]
    Shamans in vanilla do not learn this talent. The mage talent
    with this name is a separate row.

Nature's Guidance [included]
    Each rank is 1% melee hit and 1% spell hit, on every school.

Shield Specialization [included]
    Each rank is 5% more block value. Your block chance already
    includes the 1% block per rank.

Thundering Strikes [included]
    Melee crit, and your crit chance already includes it.

Tidal Mastery [included]
    Each rank is 1% nature crit for healing and lightning. Earth
    Shock is nature damage and sits outside the talent, so that one
    spell reads high.

Warlock
-------

Devastation [included]
    1% fire crit per rank. The talent covers every Destruction spell,
    including Shadow Bolt and Shadowburn. Fire crit includes it.
    Shadow crit is the whole shadow school, and the Affliction shadow
    spells are not Destruction, so shadow crit does not include
    Devastation.

Improved Searing Pain [not included]
    Searing Pain crit only, and it stacks with Devastation for that
    spell. The fire line would claim it for Immolate, Conflagrate,
    Soul Fire, Rain of Fire, and Hellfire.

Shadow Mastery [not included]
    Extra damage on the finished spell, separate from flat spell
    power. With Shadow Mastery at 5/5, spell damage still reads the
    same number on every school. Damage-percent talents such as
    Darkness work the same way.

Suppression [not included]
    Each rank is 2% against resists on Affliction spells: curses,
    Corruption, the drains, Fear, and Siphon Life. Shadow Bolt is
    Destruction. Shadow hit is the whole school.

Warrior
-------

Shield Specialization [included]
    Your block chance already includes it. Blocking with this talent
    also gives rage. Block value is unchanged.

Buffs and items
---------------

Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer [included, any class]
    +10% spell crit on every school, plus 5% melee and ranged crit
    and 140 attack power. Your melee and ranged crit already include
    that 5%.

Moonkin Aura [included]
    +3% spell crit on every school, on the druid and on nearby party
    members, while the aura is up.

Combustion [included]
    Each stack is 10% fire crit, while the buff is up.

Fire Blast [included]
    While the Fire Blast buff is on you, fire crit includes an extra
    50%.

Songflower Serenade [included, any class]
    +5% spell crit. Melee crit and ranged crit already include that
    same 5%. Strength, agility, stamina, intellect, and spirit
    already include the +15.

Slip'kik's Savvy [included, any class]
    +3% spell crit. Melee and ranged crit are unchanged.

Power of the Guardian
    Depends on who is wearing the charm.

    Mage [included]
        +2% spell crit, while the buff is on you. Hazza'rah's Charm
        of Magic grants that buff. Wearing the charm alone does not.

    Warlock [included]
        +33 spell damage and healing. That is already inside your
        spell damage.

    Priest [included]
        +62 healing, added to healing power.

    Druid [included]
        11 mana per 5, on MP5 (Buffs).

Brilliant Wizard Oil [included, any class]
    For 30 minutes, spell damage includes +36 and spell crit includes
    +1%.

Benediction [included]
    +2% holy crit while it is in your main hand.

The Ten Storms [included]
    5 pieces. +3% nature crit.

The Earthshatterer [included]
    Four pieces add 25% more mana from Mana Spring. Eight pieces add
    15 mana per 5 while Lightning Shield is up, on MP5 (Buffs). Mana
    Spring itself is on the casting totals.

Blessing of Wisdom [included, any class]
    Ranks 1-6, and Greater Blessing of Wisdom ranks 1-2. The amount
    on the buff is added to your mana while casting and while not
    casting. MP5 (Buffs) is flat mana per 5, so Wisdom is not on that
    row. The amount follows the paladin who blessed you.

Mana Spring [included, any class]
    Ranks 1-4 restore 4, 6, 8, and 10 mana every 2 seconds. Ancient
    Mana Spring restores 27. Like Blessing of Wisdom, it is added to
    mana while casting and to mana while not casting. The
    Earthshatterer 4-piece adds 25%.
