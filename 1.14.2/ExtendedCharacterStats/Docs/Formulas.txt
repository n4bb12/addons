Stats
=====

What each row on the panel means. A boss is a target three levels
above you. Talents, buffs, and items are in Sources (SOURCES.txt).

Attack power, melee crit, ranged crit, armor, defense, dodge, parry,
block chance, and attack speed come from your character. Gear,
enchants, and set bonuses are already in those numbers. Miss,
glancing, spell stats, crit reduction, and mana per 5 are calculated
with the rules under each row.

General
-------

Movement Speed
    Your run speed. While you are swimming, it is your swim speed.
    Either way it is a percent of normal running speed.

Melee
-----

Hit bonus
    Your hit, plus the melee hit talents and the hit lines on your
    gear.

Miss
    Your chance to miss a creature of your level. At equal weapon
    skill and defense that chance is 5%. Each point of defense above
    your weapon skill adds 0.1%. Once defense is more than 10 above
    your weapon skill, every point of that whole gap is worth 0.2%
    instead. Your hit comes off the result.

    In that steeper range the first 1% of hit does nothing. One point
    of hit against a boss still leaves 8% miss, and 9% hit clears it.
    Dual wield adds 19% on ordinary swings. Against a creature below
    level 10 the chance scales down with that creature's level. Miss
    stays between 0% and 60%.

Miss (Lvl + 3)
    The same chance against a boss. At 300 weapon skill and no hit,
    that is 8%.

Cat and bear
    Weapon skill counts as 5 per level. Miss is 5% against your level
    and 8% against a boss, before hit. Miss (Lvl + 3) still ignores
    the first 1% of hit.

Glancing chance
    How often a melee hit against your level, or against a boss, is a
    glancing blow. At 300 weapon skill that chance is 10% against
    your level and 40% against a boss. Each point of defense above
    your weapon skill adds 2%. Weapon skill above 5 per level does
    not lower it. The chance does not rise above 100%.

Glancing damage
    How much of your damage a glancing blow deals. At 300 weapon
    skill that blow deals 95% against your level and 65% against a
    boss.

Attack power
    Your melee attack power, buffs included.

Crit chance
    Your melee crit.

Attack speed
    Seconds per swing, main hand and off hand.

Ranged
------

Hit bonus
    Your hit, plus Surefooted, the Biznicks 247x128 Accurascope (+3%),
    hit on your gear, and ranged-only hit such as the "+1% Hit" on
    Falcon's Call.

Miss and Miss (Lvl + 3)
    The same miss table as melee, from your ranged weapon skill. Dual
    wield adds nothing here.

Attack power
    The ranged attack power vanilla gives you. A hunter is
    level × 2 + agility × 2 − 10. A rogue or warrior is
    level + agility − 10. Any other class is agility − 10. A druid in
    cat or bear has none from level and agility. Gear, enchants, and
    set bonuses such as the Cryptstalker 4-piece (+50) sit on top of
    that, along with ranged buffs: Aspect of the Hawk, Trueshot Aura,
    Earthstrike, Slayer's Crest, and each stack of Jom Gabbar. The
    client number is 0 for every class here. When it is above 0, that
    number is the row.

Crit chance
    Your ranged crit.

Attack speed
    Seconds per shot.

Defense
-------

Armor
    Your armor after diminishing returns.

Melee and ranged crit reduction
    1% for every 25 defense above 5 per level (300 at 60), plus buffs
    that make you harder to crit, and Sleight of Hand.

Spell crit reduction
    The reduction from buffs. In vanilla, defense does not reduce
    your chance to be crit by spells.

Avoidance and Avoidance (Lvl +3)
    The chance an attack of that level misses you, plus your dodge,
    parry, and block against an attacker of that level.

Enemy miss and Enemy miss (Lvl +3)
    5%, plus 0.04% for each point of your defense above the attacker's
    weapon skill. That is their chance to miss you.

Defense
    Your defense skill, as the game reports it.

Block chance
    Your block chance while a shield is equipped.

Block value
    Your shield's block, other block value on your gear, and
    strength / 20, then minus 1. Paladin Shield Specialization adds
    10% per rank and shaman Shield Specialization adds 5% per rank.
    The talent is applied before the result is cut to a whole number.
    The result is never below 0.

Parry chance
    Your parry chance, for classes that can parry.

Dodge chance
    Your dodge chance.

Note
    The dodge, parry, and block rows are against an attacker of your
    level. Each level of the attacker lowers dodge, parry, and block
    by 0.2%, so in Avoidance (Lvl +3) those three are each 0.6%
    lower than the rows above.

Spell
-----

Haste bonus
    Casting speed from buffs such as Mind Quickening, and slows such
    as Curse of Tongues.

Spell penetration
    The green line that lowers your target's resistances, plus 5 per
    rank of Arcane Subtlety. When several pieces repeat the same green
    set line, that line counts once.

Hit bonus
    Your spell hit, plus the talents for the school named on that
    line, plus the spell-hit lines on your gear. Arcane, fire, frost,
    nature, holy, shadow, and physical each have their own line.

Miss and Miss (Lvl + 3)
    Spell miss after spell hit. An equal-level target starts at 4%.
    A boss starts at 17%. Miss never falls below 1% and never rises
    above 78%.

Spell damage
    The flat bonus shared by every magic school. Percent talents stay
    separate from this number. A warlock with Shadow Mastery at 5/5
    still shows the same spell damage on every school. A priest's
    Spiritual Guidance is already in the number.

Healing power
    That shared spell damage, plus healing-only gear, plus a
    healing-only buff such as the priest Power of the Guardian. Gift
    of Nature, Spiritual Healing, and Purification multiply the heal
    when it lands.

School damage
    The total for the school named on the line. Anything above the
    shared spell damage is shown in parentheses, as in 624 (+46). A
    frost enchant is part of the frost line. Physical damage is its
    own total.

Spell crit
    Vanilla's crit chance from intellect, then your talents, gear,
    and buffs for the school named on the line. Intellect crit is
    base + intellect / (rate0 + rate1 × level). The base and the two
    rates change by class, and the second rate grows with level. At
    60 a mage is about 53.8 intellect per 1%, plus a 3.70% base, so
    382 intellect is about 10.8%. A level 1 mage is about 5%.
    Warriors, rogues, and hunters gain no spell crit from intellect.

Mana
----

MP5 (Items)
    Mana per 5 seconds from your gear, enchants, and flat set bonuses.

MP5 (Spirit)
    Mana from spirit, using vanilla's regen. A mage or priest gains
    spirit / 4 + 12.5 mana per 2 seconds. Most other classes gain
    spirit / 5 + 15. A shaman gains spirit / 5 + 17. The row turns
    that 2 second tick into mana per 5 seconds. Next to this row, the
    character sheet's spirit regen is 1 mana per second low for a
    shaman, and low for every class below level 60.

MP5 (Buffs)
    Flat mana per 5 from buffs, and 15 more from the Earthshatterer
    8-piece while Lightning Shield is up. Blessing of Wisdom and Mana
    Spring are on the casting rows below.

MP5 (Casting)
    The share of spirit regen you keep while casting, plus item mana,
    flat buff mana, Wisdom, and Mana Spring. The Earthshatterer
    4-piece adds 25% to Mana Spring. The casting share never goes
    above the full spirit regen.

MP5 (Not casting)
    Spirit regen, plus mana per 5 from items, plus mana per 5 from
    buffs, plus Blessing of Wisdom and Mana Spring.
