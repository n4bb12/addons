Extended Character Stats
========================

Extra combat stats beside your character sheet.

Open the character window. The ECS button at the bottom right shows
and hides the panel. Settings is the button at the top of the panel.
/ecs toggle and /ecs config do the same.

Defaults
--------

* Until you change a checkbox in Settings, melee is hidden for mages,
  priests, and warlocks.
* Ranged is hidden unless you can use a bow, gun, crossbow, or thrown
  weapon, and it stays hidden if you have a relic slot.
* A spell school is hidden when your class has no damage or healing
  spell of that school.
* Warriors and rogues start with the spell section hidden, and they
  have no mana section.
* Off-hand speed is shown for warriors, rogues, and a shaman with
  Dual Wield.
* Block needs a shield.
* Parry is shown for classes that can parry.

Where the numbers come from
---------------------------

Attack power, attack speed, armor, defense, dodge, parry, and block
chance come from your character. Hit, crit, miss, glancing, spell
damage, healing, spell penetration, block value, and mana per 5 are
calculated from your gear, talents, and buffs. Movement speed is 100%
plus the speed printed on your gear and buffs.
